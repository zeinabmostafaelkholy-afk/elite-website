About the project

CREEKS is an Elsewhere Developments (Mohamed Megahed Group) project on Alexandria's Ring Road. The masterplan, by Die Stadt, follows a single idea — From the Lake to the Sea: an existing lake and a continuous green spine, rather than a road grid, determine how people move and how the neighbourhoods take shape. Buildings sit within the landscape instead of beside it.

The architecture, by F&R Partnership and Laithy + Architects, speaks a contemporary Mediterranean language: quiet arches, pale stone, warm timber and generous glazing that ties each interior to its garden. The Gardens release runs from a 193 sqm Garden Flow Villa and townhouses at 205 and 208 sqm, through twin villas at 230 and 237 sqm and a 252 sqm roof-top villa, up to standalone villas at 228, 269, 334, 352 and 364 sqm — all with private gardens and parking, and maid's quarters in the larger layouts.

Gardens is the green heart of the community: shaded gardens and open lawns, dedicated cultivation plots where residents grow plants and herbs, and a small community market for exchanging what they produce — a farm-to-community way of living.

On-site life includes the Elsewhere Sporting Club (padel, pickleball, basketball, volleyball, water polo, a full gym, functional and cross training, pilates and yoga) with a Brooke House football academy and a padel academy in association with the Egyptian Padel Federation, the UK-based Brooke House College, and a business and retail district.
